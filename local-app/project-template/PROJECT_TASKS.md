## NEW TASK INTAKE — RULES

When a new {{PROJECT_NAME}} task arrives:

1. **Estimate it.** Hours, even rough. No estimate = not defined.
2. **File it.** Default to BACKLOG. Only promote to URGENT or MULTI-WEEK with a real reason.
3. **If URGENT is full and you want to add:** Something else comes off URGENT. Or wait til next week.
4. **If it's "just 10 minutes":** Fine — but log the time. Ten minutes still comes off the cap.
